What it is

MultiHelm is a self-hosted workbench (ERP) for sellers who run many Ozon stores and source products from Chinese marketplaces. It brings multi-store orders, purchase records, first-sale and tiered repricing, domestic logistics tracking, inventory and issue tickets into one system. Each customer gets a separate instance on their own server.

With many stores, these four jobs eat the day

Orders are spread across dozens of store back officesSwitching accounts to check orders and print labels is where missed and duplicated work comes from.
Purchase details live in spreadsheets and chatsFinding which order was bought where, for how much, and where the parcel is takes a long search.
Repricing is done by hand, store by storeFirst-sale, promotion and tiered prices each have their own rules. With many stores nobody can be sure every change was right.
Warehouse and operations are out of syncNobody knows a parcel has arrived, problem items are discussed in chat, and orders end up cancelled for lateness.

All stores' orders, handled on one page

Filter by account, store, status or order number. Pull new orders, sync status, print labels in bulk and split orders. Purchase platform, purchase order number, cost and courier are recorded on the order row; the page footer totals sales, known cost and estimated profit.

Repricing and promotion: preview first, then confirm

Read and process many stores together. Every write follows the same rule: preview → confirm → write once → read back to verify.

First-sale repricingA separate price rule for a product's first-sale stage.
Tiered repricingAdjust prices in tiers as orders come in, without editing store by store.
Formula pricing and automatic adsCalculate a promotion price from weight and cost; start ads, leave promotions and reprice in one run.
Favorites and ad monitoringAdd sold items to favorites, CPO ad favorites, ad overview.

Know where the goods are without asking

Domestic logistics tracking separates parcels not yet tracked, awaiting pickup, in transit and signed for, and filters orders signed for days ago but not yet shipped. Inventory, shelves and issue tickets sit in the same system, so warehouse and operations stop relaying messages in chat.

Questions and answers

Where is the data stored?

On your own server. We log in only for deployment and maintenance.

Do we have to replace our current tools?

No. Start with one or two stores in parallel and expand when it works for you.

What happens when Ozon changes its API or rules?

Adapting to API and rule changes is part of monthly maintenance, along with incident handling.

How much does it cost?

A one-time deployment fee plus a monthly maintenance fee, quoted by number of stores and modules after the demo.

Can we try it before paying in full?

Yes. Demo first, then a trial run with one or two stores. The final payment is due only after acceptance.

Is it affiliated with Ozon?

No. It is an independent third-party tool; the platform name only describes where it is used.

What language is the interface in?

Simplified Chinese at the moment.

Is it suitable for one or two stores?

No. With one or two stores a free general-purpose ERP is enough.
